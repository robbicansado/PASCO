# 1. Project Overview

PASCO (Physics Acquisition System for Computational Observation) is an open-source research project developed at the Institute of Physics of the University of São Paulo (IFUSP).

The project aims to simplify the acquisition, organization and analysis of experimental data generated in undergraduate physics laboratories. Instead of focusing exclusively on hardware or software, PASCO seeks to build a complete workflow that integrates data collection, processing, visualization and automated report generation.

The long-term objective is to provide an accessible platform that can be adopted, modified and expanded by students, instructors and researchers.

---

# 2. Vision

PASCO aims to become an open, modular and extensible platform capable of supporting undergraduate experimental physics education.

Rather than being developed as a single-purpose application, the project follows the philosophy of a continuously evolving research platform, where new modules can be incorporated without requiring a complete redesign of the system.

Every architectural decision should prioritize:

- Modularity
- Reproducibility
- Transparency
- Accessibility
- Open Science

---

# 3. Research Motivation

Laboratory classes frequently require students to collect data manually, organize measurements in spreadsheets and perform repetitive calculations before producing reports.

Although numerous digital tools exist, they are often proprietary, fragmented or designed for professional laboratories instead of educational environments.

PASCO was conceived to investigate whether modern computational tools—including automation, data visualization and artificial intelligence—can improve both the laboratory workflow and the educational experience of undergraduate students.

Beyond software development, PASCO is also intended to serve as a research platform for studying educational technologies applied to experimental physics.
